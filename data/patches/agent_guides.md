# VALORANT Tactical Agent & Post-Plant Guide

## Jett Tactical Playbook (Duelist)
Jett excels at creating immediate space on attack and holding aggressive off-angles with the Operator or Outlaw on defense.
- Attack Entry Combo: Cast Cloudburst (C) onto the primary choke or default anchor box, activate Tailwind (E) dash before peeking, and dash directly into the Cloudburst smoke. This forces defenders to turn 90 degrees while your second entry trades the kill.
- Updraft Window Entry: Combine Updraft (Q) and Tailwind (E) over high walls on Ascent A Site or Haven C Site while a teammate flashes high. Use Blade Storm (X) during eco or bonus rounds because Blade Storm has 100 percent mid-air accuracy and resets on right-click or left-click kills.
- Defensive Off-Angles: Hold tight pixel lines with Tailwind (E) primed. Take 1 shot with the Operator or Vandal and immediately dash behind hard cover to prevent refrag trades.

## Omen Tactical Playbook (Controller)
Omen provides flexible one-way smokes, vertical repositioning, and execute-winning blind utility.
- Paranoia (Q) Execution: Paranoia travels through walls up to 35 meters and nearsights anyone in its path. On Ascent A Main or Sunset B Main, throw Paranoia across the site anchor lane right as your Duelist dashes in.
- Dark Cover (E) One-Way Smokes: Place Dark Cover slightly elevated on ledges or wall crevices so defenders see attackers' feet before attackers can see out of the smoke. Dark Cover recharges every 30 seconds and lasts 15 seconds.
- Shrouded Step (C) Mindgames: Use Shrouded Step inside your own Dark Cover smoke to cross wide angles silently or fake a teleport onto elevated boxes without actually moving.

## Sova Tactical Playbook (Initiator)
Sova controls information gathering and destroys Sentinel trap setups before the team commits to a site.
- Recon Bolt (E) Timing: Fire Recon Bolt onto high back-site walls 1 second before the Duelist dashes so defenders must choose between shooting the dart (exposing their crosshair) or holding the entry player.
- Owl Drone (C) Clearing: Deploy Owl Drone to clear close rat corners and tag the site anchor. Always have a teammate follow 5 meters behind the drone to capitalize on the 2-ping tracking tag.
- Shock Bolt (Q) Anti-Sentinel Utility: Use double-bounce Shock Bolt lineups to break Cypher Trapwires on Sunset B Main or Ascent B Lane before executing.
- Hunter's Fury (X) Post-Plant: Save Hunter's Fury for post-plant spike denial from safe main corridors, combining it with a late Recon Bolt or Owl Drone ping.

## Cypher Tactical Playbook (Sentinel)
After the Patch 9.08 adjustments (0.5s Trapwire windup and 7-point Neural Theft), Cypher players must pair wires with active peek angles rather than relying on passive unbreakable setups.
- Crossfire Trapwire (C) Placement: Place Trapwires at odd diagonal angles or crouching height inside site entrances, and pair every key Trapwire with a Cyber Cage (Q). Pop the Cyber Cage the instant a wire triggers so the enemy cannot easily locate and shoot the wire during the 0.5s windup window.
- Spycam (E) Placement: Mount Spycam high above entry doorways so attackers must flick upward to break it. Tag the first entry player and swing off your Cyber Cage audio cue.
- Attack Lurking: On attack, place one flank Trapwire in Spawn or Mid and lurk up the opposite lane to cut off defender rotations.

## Post-Plant Tactical Doctrine & Economy Rules
Winning post-plant situations requires discipline, crossfire geometry, and staggered utility usage rather than solo peeking.
1. Plant Position Selection: Plant the Spike in an "Open Plant" spot visible from Main, Heaven, or Mid whenever your team controls site space. Only plant "Safe/Default" behind the box if your team lacks smoke cover and cannot cross the site.
2. Crossfire Trading Setup: Position two surviving players at a 45 to 90 degree angle relative to the Spike so if a retaking defender swings one player, the second player has an instant side-profile trade without moving.
3. Staggered Delay Utility: Never throw two mollies or Shock Bolts at the exact same time. Wait until the half-defuse audio cue (3.5 seconds into a 7.0 second full defuse), then deploy the first Hot Hands, Snake Bite, or Shock Bolt. Each delay ability buys 4 to 5 seconds of clock time.
4. Ping & Spam Discipline: After Patch 9.08 reduced Sunset B Site box spam, combine Recon Bolt or Spycam pings with high-penetration rifles (Vandal, Odin, Guardian) in short 3-bullet bursts to preserve ammo and avoid tracer return fire.
5. Economy & Buy Rules: If your team loses pistol round without planting the Spike, execute a strict Eco in Round 2 (save above 2000 credits) to guarantee a full Vandal/Phantom + Heavy Shields buy in Round 3 (3900 credits minimum).
