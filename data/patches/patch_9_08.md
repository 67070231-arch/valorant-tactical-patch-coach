# VALORANT Patch Notes 9.08

## Overview
Patch 9.08 introduces critical balance updates to Sentinel and Duelist agents alongside tactical geometry adjustments to the Sunset map. The primary focus of Patch 9.08 is reducing oppressive post-plant trap setups from Cypher, improving Phoenix entry flexibility, and opening attacker counter-play on Sunset B Site.

## Agent Updates

### Cypher
Cypher has dominated Sentinel pick rates due to unbreakable Trapwire setups and instant Spycam info gathering. Patch 9.08 adds a windup window to Trapwire and increases Ultimate cost.
- Trapwire (C): Added a 0.5 second windup delay before the tether concusses and reveals a captured enemy. Movement speed slow remains 50 percent immediately upon trigger, giving enemies a brief window to shoot the wire before full wallhack reveal takes effect. Trapwire health remains 20 hp and cost remains 200 credits.
- Neural Theft (X): Ultimate cost increased from 6 to 7 ultimate points. Neural Theft still reveals enemy locations twice with a 2 second interval after the initial scan.
- Cyber Cage (Q): Duration remains 7.25 seconds and cost remains 100 credits. Audio cue when enemies pass through the cage has been made 15 percent louder for nearby defenders.

### Phoenix
Phoenix receives major quality-of-life buffs in Patch 9.08 to compete with movement Duelists like Jett and Raze.
- Curveball (Q): Moved to Phoenix signature ability slot. Players spawn with 1 free Curveball charge each round and earn 1 recharged flash after getting 2 kills. Maximum charges remain 2, secondary charge costs 250 credits, and flash blind duration increased from 1.1 seconds to 1.25 seconds.
- Blaze (C): Moved to basic ability slot costing 150 credits. Phoenix can now cast Blaze through walls and solid terrain, allowing safe vision denial before executing onto a site. Healing from Blaze remains 50 hp over 4 seconds.
- Hot Hands (E): Moved to basic ability slot costing 200 credits with 1 charge. Healing remains 50 hp over 4 seconds and molly damage remains 60 dps.

### Gekko
Gekko globule pickup mechanics have been tuned to reward coordinated team aggression rather than passive spamming.
- Globules (Passive): World duration of Dormant Globules for Dizzy (E), Wingman (Q), and Thrash (X) reduced from 20 seconds to 15 seconds. Reclaim channel time increased from 1.0 second to 1.5 seconds.
- Dizzy (E): Plasma blast projectile speed reduced by 10 percent, and active air duration reduced from 1.5 seconds to 1.2 seconds.
- Wingman (Q): Concuss cone angle widened by 15 percent, making close-corner clears more reliable.
- Thrash (X): Ultimate cost increased from 7 to 8 ultimate points.

### Yoru
Yoru receives consistency improvements against visual clutter effects.
- Fakeout (C): Decoy clone now properly displays blind and concuss animations when hit by enemy abilities such as Skye Guiding Light, Gekko Dizzy, or Breach Fault Line.
- Dimensional Drift (X): Reduced equip delay when exiting ultimate from 0.8 seconds to 0.7 seconds.

## Map Updates

### Sunset
Sunset B Site post-plant meta relied heavily on spamming through the central pillar from B Main while protected by unbreakable Cypher wires. Patch 9.08 overhauls B Site cover and Mid Courtyard sightlines.
- B Site Central Pillar: Replaced the wide front box on B Site with a narrower non-spammable metal crate, reducing safe angles for B Main post-plant spam.
- B Site Back Platform: Extended the back-site platform cover toward B Boba, giving defenders a stronger anchor position during site hits and retakes.
- B Main Exterior: Removed the deep cubby near B Main entrance to simplify defender clearing utility.
- Mid Courtyard: Shifted the central radianite box slightly toward Mid Tiles to break long-range Operator sightlines from Attacker Spawn into Mid Bottom.

## Map Rotation & Competitive Updates
- Competitive Queue Map Pool: Pearl and Split re-enter the active 7-map competitive rotation in Patch 9.08.
- Removed Maps: Icebox and Lotus exit the competitive and Premier map pools to undergo layout refinements.
- Active Competitive Pool in 9.08: Ascent, Bind, Haven, Sunset, Abyss, Pearl, and Split.
- Weapon Combat Report: Fixed a bug where Phantom and Vandal wall penetration damage occasionally rounded up by 1 damage point in the post-round summary panel.
